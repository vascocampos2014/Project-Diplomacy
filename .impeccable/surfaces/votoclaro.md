Scope: the VotoClaro web app (Expo web), every tab and detail screen. Mode: Operate.
Audience: young voters on a phone who don't know politics. Task: see what was voted lately, the result, and who voted how, in seconds.
Constraints: user palette (#F8F9FA primary, #232323 secondary, #120A8F and #449DD1 accents), logo image as-is, neutrality, AA contrast, pt-PT copy.
Memorable moment: the engraved hemicycle sweeps across the 230 seats and the result stamp settles in its centre.
Unresolved: building images are being generated by the user (Higgsfield); native (iOS/Android) pass comes later.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Parliament's votes as a Portuguese front page: azulejo tiles and landmark engravings say "Portugal" at a glance, and every result is drawn in ink on the chamber's own shape. It refuses traffic-light vote colours and the generic civic-tech card stack.

OWN-WORLD: Bright Snow paper; Ultramarine azulejo bands (geometry, SVG) with a landmark engraving framed in a tile window per tab; big ExtraBold Ultramarine headlines; soft 14px white cards with hairlines; pill filters; solid Carbon primary buttons and soft grey secondary ones. Votes in ink only: solid (a favor), hatched (contra), empty (abstenção), grey (ausentes). Public Sans throughout.

STORY: The visitor lands on the latest vote as a lead story (headline, hemicycle, stamp, who voted how), scans the recent votes as cards with mini hemicycles, opens one for the full chamber, then browses deputies, asks a question, or compares parties.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with logo; the São Bento window over the tile band (tile band alone until the image exists); "Votações" title and theme pills; the lead vote's tag, Ultramarine headline and the engraved hemicycle with its stamp.

FORM: Chosen by the user over four mockups (Jornal, Ultramarino, Selo, Azulejo) on 2026-11-14, with the engraved hemicycle (version G) for results. Signature interaction: the hemicycle sweep (700ms, exponential ease-out) followed by the stamp settling (400ms at 560ms). Motion grammar: transitions.dev tokens; entrances via [data-anim]; reduced motion shows everything complete.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; building rasters must carry their provenance in assets/edificios/README.md.
